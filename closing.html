MONTH 1
COMPLETE

One month down. I already miss you when you're not on the call.

A month ago I didn't know your name past a Snapchat username. Now my mornings start with your good morning and my nights don't feel finished without your good night.

Thank you for the four tries it took to get me to say yes to coffee. Thank you for listening to me talk non-stop and never once looking bored. Thank you for the chai on the day I hadn't eaten anything, for coming to the temple every single time without being asked, for feeding me first at every date, and for a rose that didn't need an occasion.

I don't know what the next month looks like, or the one after that. But I know I want you there for it — gadgets, travel plans, chaos and all.

— the girl who talks too much

Here's to many more of these.
