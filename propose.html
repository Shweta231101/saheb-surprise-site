WHY I'M SURE ABOUT YOU

A few reasons this doesn't feel like "only a month."

☕

It took four tries to get me to say yes to coffee. You didn't give up on the third.

🫖

You showed up with tea on a day I hadn't eaten a single thing. Nobody asked you to.

🛕

You come to the temple with me every time, without being asked twice.

🍽️

On every date, you feed me first and eat after. Every single time.

👂

You listen to me talk non-stop, about nothing in particular, and never once make me feel like too much.

🌹

You gave me a rose with absolutely no occasion attached to it.

🌙

My day only feels complete with your good morning at the start and your good night at the end.

I know it's only been a month. But I already know how I feel about you — so here's my truth, this early: will you marry me?

Not today, not tomorrow. Just, someday — with you.
